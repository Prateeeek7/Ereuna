# EREUNA

**Research, on your radar.**

Ereuna turns a research topic into a map of what the field knows. Type a topic, or upload your own papers, and Ereuna finds the relevant research, reads it, and shows you the findings, the disagreements and the open questions, with the source behind every claim.

It is available as an **Android app** and a **website**, both using one account.

---

## The problem

A literature review still means weeks of reading PDFs by hand.

- **Too many papers.** Thousands of results per topic, and no clear place to start.
- **Findings are buried.** Results and metrics sit deep inside PDFs, each in its own format.
- **Conflicts stay hidden.** Papers that disagree are rarely read side by side.
- **AI without proof.** Chatbot summaries can invent claims and cite no source.

Students and researchers need a fast, trustworthy way to see what a field already knows, where studies disagree and what is still open, without reading every paper first.

## What you get: a research map

Each map brings together:

| Part | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Papers** | Up to 25 ranked papers from open scholarly sources |
| **Findings** | Key results, each with the exact sentence it came from |
| **Conflicts** | Where papers report different results |
| **Gaps** | Open questions, with suggested experiments to close them |
| **Metrics** | Reported numbers compared side by side |
| **Tools** | Datasets, models and methods the papers use |
| **Citations** | How the papers cite each other |

## How it works

1. **Search.** Open sources such as OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar and arXiv are searched for your topic.
2. **Rank and read.** The most relevant papers are ranked, and their open full text is fetched.
3. **Extract.** AI pulls out findings, metrics, methods and limitations from each paper.
4. **Verify.** Every extracted item is checked against the paper itself and keeps its quote, page and section. Anything that can't be traced back to the paper is left out.
5. **Compare.** The papers are compared to find agreements, conflicts, gaps and shared tools.
6. **Graph and suggest.** A citation graph is built, and experiments are drafted for the gaps.

You watch the map build step by step. A topic someone has already mapped opens instantly.

### Your own papers

Prefer to start from papers you already have? Upload up to 15 PDFs and Ereuna builds a map from them, optionally adding related papers it finds. Your PDFs are read and then discarded, never stored, and the resulting map is visible only to you.

## Exploring a map

- **Overview:** a short synthesis of the field and the map's key numbers.
- **Findings:** every result with its source quote, page and section.
- **Compare:** a side-by-side table of papers and their reported metrics.
- **Conflicts and gaps:** where the research disagrees and what is still unanswered.
- **Graph views:** connections between papers, the citation network, a timeline, and a chart of reported values.
- **Paper pages:** the details and evidence for any single paper.
- **Exports:** take a map into your own writing as Markdown, BibTeX or CSV.
- **Refresh:** re-run a topic map later to pull in newer papers.

## One account, two places

- **Android app:** search or upload on your phone, watch maps build live, and keep maps you've opened on the device.
- **Website:** the same search and upload, with larger graph views, the compare table and exports, in light or dark theme.

Sign in once and everything follows you. Every map you build is saved to your **Library** automatically, and the Library stays in sync between the app and the website. You can also save individual papers there.

## Built on open data

Paper metadata comes from **OpenAlex**, **Semantic Scholar**, **arXiv** and **Crossref**. Ereuna links to each paper's original source so you can always read the full work.

## Privacy

- Your email is used only to sign you in.
- Uploaded PDFs are processed in memory and discarded.
- You can delete your account at any time, from the app, the website or the account deletion page. This removes your library and every map built from your uploads.
